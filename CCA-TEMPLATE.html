The name is the model. We connect the food industry — four ways.

01 To knowledge

Bootcamps, workshops, and training that teach what running a food business actually takes.

02 To experts

A faculty of working chefs, operators, and specialists — not career lecturers.

03 To community

A growing network of owners and teams who learn, share, and grow alongside each other.

04 To industry

Partnerships that link food businesses with the brands and suppliers that serve them.

How we work with you

Two ways in — one team behind both.

Most food businesses meet us in a workshop. When you're ready to go deeper, the same experts come in to work on your operation directly.

01

Bootcamps & Events

The front door

Time-boxed, hands-on programs open to any food-business owner. Learn a specific skill, meet our faculty, and leave with something you can use on Monday.

  • Public bootcamps like Beyond the Plate
  • Summits and industry gatherings
  • Short workshops and masterclasses
Best for owners and teams building their skills.
02

Consultancy

  • Available whenever you reach out
Best for businesses ready to fix or scale an operation.
